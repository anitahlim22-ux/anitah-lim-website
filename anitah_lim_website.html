About me

A community-focused health practitioner

I am Anitah Lim, a health practitioner with over three years of experience. I value compassionate communication, health education, and helping people understand available resources so they can make informed decisions.

My work and interests in reproductive health are guided by dignity, privacy, cultural sensitivity, and respect for each person's circumstances.
